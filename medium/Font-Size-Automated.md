### Introduction

Most of the time. All of us glimpse at typography when we are about to proceed with a new website project. We choose one or two font-size online, bring them in our project, and globally include their style. We presume we are all fit for basic settings. Adding code at a steady scale, matching contents on a specific context, simulating and reviewing the result on screen.

From school and self-teaching we are constantly writing. This habit takes in a sense of acquaintance where we already decide as trivial any matter on which concern is emphasized in text. However the first principle that modern web design points up is : typography. Even more than colors and layout, or contrast. Because clarity, readability and simplicity are the essence and soul of any solution oriented online.

Because this subject is almost self-centered on any of our decisions. The rising problems live on the methods, tips and processes that will assist to deliver fast and professional designs that will pass media queries pushes.
