A few years later, Microsoft released their first Windows Operating Systems : “Windows V1.0” with an optimized mouse-driven graphical interface.

### IV/ The Unfamiliar Power of Linux

Linux for full access and main control on your system will demand you register as a “Superuser”. Why is it so Important ?
For e.g : If someone is willing to delete a file inadvertently or intentionally, and that file gets encrypted with permission or are core files of the system, or even an app installed permanently. This user will be asking the right to do such an action by entering the **secret password** of the superuser before the action proceeds. That avoids a lot of stress when you hand your computer to someone else in need, or deal with people that intentionally want to hurt you by screwing your work. That’s why when you register as SuperUser you don’t share your password with anyone. You keep it secret between you and Linux.

Linux provides quite a lot of command lines, and have active communities on the net where you can ask for solving the specific huge problem you are dealing with within your computer and that the usage of a couple of redirected command lines have not yet solved. The kind that makes you worried about losing data in your drive or damaging your CPU, or network card (for e.g). And help you return your device or computer to a state where it was working fine. So that you can continue with your tasks ensuring you spend a least amount of money.

Linux offers you the capability to create a “Live USB Drive.” A kind of USB drive you can plug in any remote computer allows you to open your linux distribution and continue to work from where you left at home or at the office, and register the change inside the internal part of the USB made as Disk storage. Quite an Amazing and Magical gadget to pull in your pocket.

These are just a few things that we can uplift, there are a bunch of many others.
